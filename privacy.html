Privacy Policy — Isaac Video App

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Isaac Video App is a private, invite-only app for a single family to record, share, and watch each other's videos. It is designed for use by children under the supervision of a parent or guardian. This policy explains how the app handles information.

The short version

Your videos and family activity are stored locally or in your family's private Apple iCloud, not on a developer-operated content server. There are no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking. If you choose to email support or report a concern, support receives your email address and the information you send.

What the app stores, and where

Your videos and family activity are stored in one of two places, both under your control:

  • On your device: videos you are recording or editing, and small preferences such as your chosen display name and channel avatar.
  • In your own Apple iCloud (via Apple's CloudKit): videos you publish, together with the reactions, comments, and display names of family members you have invited. This data lives in your iCloud account and shared iCloud space. The developer of this app has no access to it and cannot read it.

Camera and microphone

The app uses the camera and microphone when you choose to record a video, add a voiceover, or make a parent's voice note. Original videos and voiceovers stay on the device while you edit. Published videos, voiceovers, and any send-back voice note are stored in the family's private iCloud sharing. The app does not stream recordings.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for family use with children. It does not collect personal information from children, does not contain advertising or in-app purchases, and does not expose public web or social-media browsing. Concern reporting is available on the family-viewer and parent surfaces, not in the child's read-only video player. A parent or guardian controls who is invited and approves videos before they are shared within the family.

Optional concern reports and support email

“Report a concern” prepares an email to jason@rams.land with your chosen reason, optional details, and a video reference when available. The app does not attach private videos, photos, titles, comments, or account information. Nothing is sent until you send the email in your email service. Your email service also supplies your sender address and may include your name or signature. Please avoid including children's names or other sensitive information.

Reports are support correspondence outside your private CloudKit channel; they do not grant support access to that channel. “Copy report” places the text on this device's clipboard for up to ten minutes, without Universal Clipboard sharing. Opening the form alone does not transmit a report.

Data sharing and deletion

Content is shared only with the specific family members a parent invites through Apple's private sharing. You can delete any video from within the app. Because all data is held in your own Apple iCloud, you can also remove it at any time through your Apple ID / iCloud settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to jason@rams.land.
