Isaac Video App Support

A private, family-only place for making and sharing videos.

Getting started

Isaac Video App lets a child record, decorate, and share videos with invited family members. A parent manages sharing and moderation from the parent controls.

Common questions

  • Who can see videos? Only family members invited through the app's private sharing flow can view them.
  • How do I join a family channel? Ask the parent who manages the channel to send you an invitation.
  • Why can't I change parent settings? Parent controls are protected with a PIN.
  • How do I report a concern? Choose “Report a concern” while viewing a video, at the bottom of the family feed, or in Parent → Settings. Choose a reason, optionally explain what happened, then continue in your email app and send the report to support. If email will not open, use “Copy report” and paste it into your preferred email service.

Need more help?

For app support, email jason@rams.land. For invitation or family-channel questions, contact the parent who invited you. Please do not include a child's name, videos, or other personal information in your message.

What a concern report shares

The app prepares your chosen reason, optional details, and a video reference when reporting from a video. It does not attach the video, its title, comments, or account information. You review and send the email yourself; support receives your email address and what you choose to send. Support cannot browse private CloudKit channels. For immediate moderation or access changes, contact the parent who invited you.

Isaac Video App is designed for private family sharing.
