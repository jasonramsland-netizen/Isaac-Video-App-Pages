Isaac's World Support

A private, family-only place for making and sharing videos.

Getting started

Isaac's World lets a child record, decorate, and share videos with invited family members. A parent manages sharing and moderation from the parent controls.

Common questions

  • Who can see videos? Only family members invited through the app's private sharing flow can view them.
  • How do I join a family channel? Ask the parent who manages the channel to send you an invitation.
  • Why can't I change parent settings? Parent controls are protected with a PIN.
  • How do I report a problem? Contact the parent who shared the app with you. They can help with invitations and app settings.

Need more help?

For help with this family app, please contact the parent or family member who invited you. Do not share a child's name, videos, or other personal information in public messages.

Isaac's World is designed for private family sharing.
